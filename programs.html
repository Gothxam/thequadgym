01

Build muscle and get stronger.

Weight training

BeginnersMusclePowerlifting

Squat racks, Olympic bars and plates, benches and a dumbbell wall. Your trainer writes down a plan with the weights and reps, then checks it every few weeks so you can see the numbers go up.

  • Squat racks, Olympic bars and plates
  • Dumbbell wall plus machines
  • A written plan, checked regularly
  • Form corrected free of charge

02

Lose weight without a crash diet.

Cardio and fat loss

Weight lossStaminaCircuits

Treadmills, cross trainers, cycles and a rower, plus circuits on the floor. Your trainer sets targets around normal home food so the plan is one you can actually follow.

  • Treadmills, cross trainers, cycles, rower
  • Circuit and interval training
  • Diet guidance around home food
  • Weight check every few weeks

03

Dance cardio that does not feel like cardio.

Zumba

Group classAll levelsIncluded

Instructor-led classes in the studio, with mirrors and a sound system. Most people walk in with no dance background. You pick up the steps in about a week.

  • Instructor-led group classes
  • All levels, no experience needed
  • Separate studio, away from the weights
  • Included in your membership

04

Easy on the joints, good for stamina.

Aerobics

Low impactMobilityIncluded

Low-impact classes that build endurance and keep you moving well. A good place to start if you are coming back after a break or want something kinder on the knees.

  • Low impact and joint friendly
  • Builds stamina and mobility
  • Good after a long break
  • Included in your membership

05

Training that helps outside the gym.

Functional training

AthletesPowerEndurance

Turf lane, cables, kettlebells, ropes and bodyweight work, run as circuits. Built for athletes, for physical jobs, and for anyone who wants to be capable rather than just look it.

  • Turf lane for sled work and carries
  • Cables, kettlebells and ropes
  • Circuit-style sessions
  • Sport conditioning on request

06

One trainer, your own plan.

Personal training

One to oneCustom planCosts extra

The quickest route if you have a date to hit, or if you want to stop guessing. Your trainer builds the plan around your schedule, any injuries and your food, and changes it as you go.

  • A plan built only for you
  • Diet plan around your routine
  • Every session supervised
  • One and three month packages
