Questions

Things people ask.

01What are the timings?

Open 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. Closed Sunday. Busiest before 8 AM and after 6 PM. Between 11 AM and 4 PM the floor is nearly empty.

02What does ₹4,999 cover?

Six months of membership, plus one month you can put on hold whenever you need it. It covers the gym, cardio, turf area and all classes including Zumba and aerobics. Personal training is the only thing charged separately.

03Can I pause my membership?

Yes, one month free. Tell us at the desk before you travel and we stop the clock.

04I have never trained before. Is that a problem?

No. On your first day a trainer shows you around, explains each machine and writes you a simple routine. Most of our members started with no experience.

05Do you have personal training?

Yes. One trainer, your own plan and diet, every session supervised. Sold in one and three month packages. Call for the current price.

06Is there a ladies batch?

Women train on the main floor through the day, and the studio runs separate Zumba and aerobics batches. Call us for this week’s timings.

07What should I bring?

Training shoes, a towel and a water bottle. Lockers and changing rooms are there if you are coming from work.
