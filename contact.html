Before you call

Quick answers.

01Do I need to book?

No. Walk in any time between 5 AM and 10 PM, Monday to Saturday. Someone will show you around.

02What should I bring on day one?

Training shoes, a towel and a water bottle. Lockers and changing rooms are available.

03Can I pay in instalments?

Talk to us at the desk and we will tell you what is possible for the plan you want. Nothing gets added later.

04What are the Zumba and aerobics timings?

They change through the year. Call or WhatsApp and we will send the current schedule.

05Do you offer a trial?

Come in, look around and talk to a trainer at no cost. Ask at the desk about trying a session.
