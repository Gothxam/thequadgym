Our story

We built the gym we wanted to train in.

Plenty of places will sell you a membership. Fewer will notice when your back rounds on the third set. That is the difference we aim for.

More than five thousand people have trained here: students, people on shifts, mothers coming back after a break, men in their fifties told by a doctor to start moving. Everyone gets the same floor and the same help.

In 2026 we shut the gym and rebuilt it. New floor, better lighting and ventilation, new machines, a proper class studio and clean changing rooms.

How we run it

Six rules we stick to.

1

Trainers stay on the floor

Not in an office. If your form slips, someone fixes it that set, whether or not you pay for personal training.

2

Machines get fixed fast

We check the equipment every week. A jammed pin is a machine nobody can use.

3

No pushy selling

Nobody will corner you about supplements or an upgrade. Ask us and we will tell you if it is worth the money.

4

Beginners come first

Day one you get a tour, a routine and someone to ask. You should not have to pretend you know how a cable machine works.

5

The floor stays clean

Weights go back on the rack, benches get wiped, washrooms are checked through the day.

6

Everyone trains together

Seventeen-year-olds and sixty-year-olds, first-timers and serious lifters. Nobody gets priority.
